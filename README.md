Morphs FocusFlow v5.0 
Air-Gapped Ambient Sound Synthesizer, Studio MP3 Deck & Fullscreen OLED HUD

Transform your workspace into a calm, focused environment. Morphs FocusFlow is an offline ambient workstation designed to help you study, work, meditate, and relax. Engineered with mathematical sound generation, a local audio player, a decoupled Pomodoro desk timer, and fullscreen landscape clock displays, FocusFlow keeps you centered and productive without distractions.

🌊 Real-Time Procedural Soundscapes

Leave audio loops and low-bitrate recordings behind. FocusFlow synthesizes natural, calming soundscapes directly inside your device using real-time Web 

Audio synthesis:

  - Binaural Alpha Waves: Tuned for mental clarity, steady focus, and relaxed awareness.
  - Cosmic Drone: Deep harmonic sub-bass vibrations for immersive focus and deep meditation.
  - Night Crickets: Rhythmic, atmospheric evening chirps.
  - Soft Fan & Motor Hum: Smooth low-frequency white noise to mask distracting office sounds.
  - Ocean Waves: Organic, dynamic surf swells that ebb and flow naturally.
  - Rain Shower: Continuous rainfall texture designed to drown out room echo.
  - Alpine Wind: Dynamic mountain breezes with subtle resonant gusts.
  - Wind Chimes: A randomized pentatonic chime bell pattern for mindfulness.

🎵 Built-In Studio MP3 Deck

Prefer your own study playlists, podcasts, or lo-fi beats? Load any audio file directly from your local device:

  - Frameless 3-Button Transport: Jump back 15 seconds, play/pause, and skip forward 15 seconds.
  - Interactive Seeker: Real-time scrubbing slider with live timecodes and duration displays.
  - Dual Volume Balance: Independent control over track volume and master output gain for balanced mixing over ambient background soundscapes.

⏱️ Decoupled Focus Timer & Gentle Wake Alarm

  - 4 Focus Intervals: Instant access to 25-minute Deep Focus, 5-minute Short Rest, 15-minute Long Rest, and customizable minute steppers.
  - 5-Slot Scenes Hub: Save snapshots of your favorite sound mixes, timer lengths, and volume balances into 5 instant-recall slots.
  - Gentle Wake Alarm: Set your target morning or study wake-up time. The alarm swells softly over 25 seconds—waking you up gently with harmonious Solfeggio tones or your favorite saved ambient scene.

🕰️ Dual Fullscreen OLED HUDs (Desk Timer & Landscape Clock)

Turn your phone or tablet into an aesthetic desk clock while you work:

  - True 7-Segment Digital Clock: Authentic digital timepiece styling with an optional digital glow switch.
  - Multiple Typographic Styles: Switch between Digital 7-Segment, Modern Sans, Digital Mono, Slim Minimal, and Neon Cyber displays on both Clock and Desk screens.
  - 5 Curated Scenic Backdrops: Hand-crafted vector landscapes including Alpine Twilight, Golden Sunset Balcony, Emerald Forest Canopy, Midnight Crescent Moon, and Pure Minimalist OLED Black.
  - Triple OLED Dimmer: 4 quick brightness presets (100%, 50%, 20%, 5%) plus granular scrubbing and auto-dimming idle protection to keep OLED screens safe at night.

🔒 100% Offline & Completely Private

  - Zero Telemetry: No user tracking, no third-party analytics, and no accounts required.
  - Air-Gapped & Battery Efficient: Runs locally on your device with no internet connection required.
  - Hardware Shield: Integrated top dead-zone safe buffer to keep the workstation controls clear of camera notches and hole-punch displays.
